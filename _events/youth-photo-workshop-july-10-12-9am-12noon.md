For youth ages 9-17, this workshop will cover basic photography concepts, shooting, selecting and sharing of photographs taken during the workshop. Only basic devices (simple camera, phone or tablets are all fine) needed, the focus is on "Learning to See." Complete pre-registration below to sign up! Must be pre-registered by July 7th **REGISTRATION NOW CLOSED**. A confirmation email will be sent with more information. Youth need to arrive at the Learning Center no later than 9am each day and are committed to all 3 days upon enrollment. This workshop is ***totally free*** and all printing, etc costs will be done by FoBB.

\*\* Youth must be familiar with their device and will need to copy from camera card, email or text 3 photos each day for work submission. FoBB will provide basic internet access but is not responsible for any data or messaging charges from the child's phone/device.
