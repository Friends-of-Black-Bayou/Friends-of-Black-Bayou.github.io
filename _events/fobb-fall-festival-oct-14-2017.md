A little birdie has told us ... the date for the annual Friends of Black Bayou Lake NWR Fall Celebration has been decided! Mark your calendars for Saturday, October 14, 2017 from 10AM to 3PM and get ready for a wonder-filled, fun day at the refuge. Make plans to attend.

As always, there will be food, games, canoe trips, education, and winning photos on display from the Photo Contest, and lots more.

If you are interested in setting up a booth, or want to participate, please contact FoBB president, Kris Kelley to let him know at (318) 680-6411 or [kelltopia@gmail.com](mailto:kelltopia@gmail.com).
