**10AM (ages 3-5)**\
Monthly kid's program. We will be learning about fish, using paint and doing fish printing so may be very messy.

**11AM (ages 6-10)**\
Monthly kid's program this month focusing on fish. We will be doing fish printing which is rather messy and also backyard bass to test casting skills.

Session to be led by Refuge ... Nova Clarke.

This event is FREE to the public.\
This event is put on by the national wildlife refuge operated by the US Fish and Wildlife Service. The refuge is open 7 days a week during daylight hours.

There is no charge to visit the refuge - the only charge is to launch a boat.
