Winners of the 2023 Friends of Black Bayou photo contest have been named. The Charlie Heck Memorial Best of Show went to Jeff Perot for “Patches.”

All photos in the contest were taken at the North Louisiana Refuges Complex, which includes Black Bayou Lake, D’Arbonne, Upper Ouachita, Tensas River, and Handy Brake National Wildlife Refuges.
