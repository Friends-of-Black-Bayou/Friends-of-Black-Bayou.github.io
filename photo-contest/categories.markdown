The contest has nine categories, including three categories added in 2026&mdash;Arthropods, Fungi, and Herps and Fish&mdash;, and, starting in 2026, the category previously called Animals is now Mammals. If you have entered before, it is worth reading through these before you choose where to submit.

Each category has two examples: one demonstrates the standard and the other demonstrates one or more common mistakes.
