Study nature, as the more you know, the more you'll see! Anticipate opportunities. Be stealthy. The best wildlife camera is the one you have with you. Golden hours (early morning and evening) are optimal for photographing wildlife as creatures are preoccupied with feeding and come closer, and the light quality is outstanding. Be quick! Seconds count! Check your results on site, to remedy issues for images that can be re-shot.

Blue light 20 minutes before dawn or after sunset can be nicely moody, but normally we can only be on the refuges from half an hour before sunrise to half an hour after sunset, so the time is tight. Take advantage of weather conditions, periods after rain, seasonal opportunities, special programs that allow night visitation, Full-Moon Walks & Frog Walks, etc.

Use your pixels wisely! With your subject's well-being in mind, try to fill the frame with your subject without having to crop-in too much. Move your camera to try new perspectives and vary orientation. Check that your subject is in sharp focus, animals' eyes should be in focus ideally, except in unusual circumstances.

## Mobile Phone Cameras

Practice your close-up technique on small items to hone your technique. If you are using a cell phone camera, set the image quality high when you have a promising subject (Same with video settings.). The default setting isn't good enough for entries. 

With your subject's well-being in mind, try to fill the frame with subjects without using so much electronic zoom that it degrades the picture.

## Digital cameras

Don't use Automatic White Balance (AWB kills natural color), set your White Balance to Cloudy or Shade to capture the subtlety of ambient light. For best detail set as low ISO as your safe shutter speed will allow for your lens length, use camera/lens stabilization and support your camera to avoid shake. Try modifying your settings for sharpness, saturation and contrast in-camera and use your preferred settings.

Shooting RAW format and editing later gives you the greatest scope of options.
