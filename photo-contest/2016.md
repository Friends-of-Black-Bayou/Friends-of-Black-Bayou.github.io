Winners of the twelfth annual Friends of Black Bayou photo contest were named on October 8, 2016. Best in Show went to Mohan Krishna for "Deer on a Railway Track."

All photos in the contest were taken at the North Louisiana Refuges Complex, including Black Bayou Lake, D'Arbonne, Upper Ouachita, and Handy Brake National Wildlife Refuges.
