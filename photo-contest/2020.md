All photos in the contest were taken at the North Louisiana Refuges Complex, including Black Bayou Lake, D’Arbonne, Upper Ouachita, Tensas River, and Handy Brake National Wildlife Refuges. FoBB board member Leslie Calhoun chaired the contest, with assistance from Ann Bloxom Smith. Judges were regional photographers who evaluated the entries in the youth and adult categories. Charles Heck, Sr., founded the contest in 2004.
