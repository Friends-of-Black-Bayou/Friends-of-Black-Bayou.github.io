Best of Show in the 2024 Annual Photo Contest went to Kathy Dage for "Target in Sight."

To be considered, each photo had to be taken at the North Louisiana Refuges Complex, which includes Black Bayou Lake, D'Arbonne, Upper Ouachita, Tensas River, and Handy Brake National Wildlife Refuges.
