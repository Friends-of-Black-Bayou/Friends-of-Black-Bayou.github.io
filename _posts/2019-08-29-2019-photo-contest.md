We welcome you as a participant in the annual Friends of Black Bayou, Inc. Photo Contest held each October in conjunction with National Wildlife Refuge Week. The photo contest is open to all photographic skill levels. FoBB is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, supporting the protection and enhancement of Black Bayou Lake National Wildlife Refuge. This photographic competition is an opportunity to promote and encourage an appreciation of wildlife and the scenic natural settings that abound within the North Louisiana Refuges Complex, consisting of Black Bayou Lake, D'Arbonne, Handy Brake and Upper Ouachita National Wildlife Refuges. If you have questions, please call 318-387-1114 or email [friendsofblackbayou@gmail.com](mailto:friendsofblackbayou@gmail.com) .
