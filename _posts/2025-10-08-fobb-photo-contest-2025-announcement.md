Congratulations and thank you to all the great photographers who entered 220+ pictures in this year's FoBB Photo Contest, an annual event organized originally in 2003 by Charlie Heck, Sr.

First-place winning photos will be displayed in the Refuge Visitor Center as soon as possible, and all ribboned photos will be displayed on the webpage: [Annual Photo Contest - FRIENDS OF BLACK BAYOU LAKE NATIONAL WILDLIFE REFUGE](https://www.friendsofblackbayou.org/photocontest.html) in a slideshow pdf[.](http://www.friendsofblackbayou.org/?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTAAYnJpZBExWWZuWXlrSE9Cd0ZWZFYydAEeScXxkZwAq-rcIHc1_fQZFJkOEmZ7it_7hfGbpvbiTGeX24B12Yuk4gfxPnI_aem_y46FW3Q9jFm87iafYoR_TA)

Our thanks to the contest chairs, Leslie Calhoun and Kimberly Paxton, along with the anonymous judges.

The Charles Heck Memorial Grand Prize winner this year was taken by amateur photographer Tony Tramontana and is entitled "Nature Provides"
