**\*\* US FISH & WILDLIFE EVENT LED BY NOVA CLARKE \*\***\
2017 FWS Summer Camps Registration will begin on March 14, 2017. Dates for the camps are:

**June 12 to 16**\
8:30 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.\
Completed Grades K, 1 or 2

**June 19 to 23rd**\
8:30 a.m. to 12 noon\
Completed Grades 3 or 4

**June 26-27**\
8:30 a.m. to 12 noon\
Fishing/Hunting Camp\
Learn safe hunting & fishing techniques and safety\
Ages 8-16

For more information or to register, please contact Nova Clarke at [Nova_Clarke@fws.gov](mailto:Nova_Clarke@fws.gov) or 318-387-1114
